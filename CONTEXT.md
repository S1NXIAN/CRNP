# CONTEXT.md — Crates N' Plates glossary

- **Pending**: online order placed by customer, awaiting cashier acceptance. Never shown to chef.
- **Accepted**: order accepted by cashier and waiting to be cooked. Includes walk-in orders. Left column of kitchen screen.
- **Cooking**: order chef has claimed and is actively cooking. Right column of kitchen screen. Covers `preparing` and stranded `ready` rows.
- **Done**: order chef finished. Leaves kitchen screen, appears in history. Cashier handles everything after.
- **Shared kitchen**: one kitchen display, one cooking column, used by whoever is on shift. No per-chef ownership.
